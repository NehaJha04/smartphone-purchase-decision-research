# Consumer Smartphone Purchase Decision Survey

## Survey Purpose

This questionnaire is designed to understand the factors that influence consumers when purchasing smartphones.

The survey focuses on consumer preferences, purchasing behavior, price sensitivity, brand preferences, product attributes, online reviews, and recommendations.

The responses will be used for an independent market research study and portfolio project.

| Q26 | Consumer expectations | Qualitative thematic analysis |

# Data Quality Rules

The following rules will be applied during data cleaning:

1. Remove respondents who do not meet the age requirement.
2. Remove respondents who do not own or have previously purchased a smartphone.
3. Remove respondents who do not provide consent.
4. Check for duplicate submissions.
5. Identify incomplete responses.
6. Check for inconsistent responses.
7. Review unusually fast or low-quality responses where response-time information is available.
8. Document all exclusions before conducting final analysis.

---

# Expected Survey Output

The final dataset should contain one row per valid respondent and one column for each survey variable.

The survey data will subsequently be exported to CSV format and used for:

- Data cleaning
- Exploratory data analysis
- Descriptive statistics
- Cross-tabulation
- Statistical hypothesis testing
- Consumer segmentation
- Visualization
- Business recommendations

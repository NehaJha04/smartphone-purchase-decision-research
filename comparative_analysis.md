# Comparative Analysis

## Purpose

The comparative analysis examines whether consumer characteristics and purchasing behavior vary across different consumer groups.

The analysis focuses on relationships identified in the research hypotheses.

---

## Analysis 1 — Age and Brand Preference

### Research Question

Does smartphone brand preference differ across age groups?

### Variables

- Age group
- Current smartphone brand

### Planned Analysis

Cross-tabulation will be used to compare the distribution of smartphone brands across age groups.

A Chi-square test of independence will subsequently be considered to determine whether the relationship between age group and brand preference is statistically significant.

---

## Analysis 2 — Income and Price Sensitivity

### Research Question

Is income level associated with consumers' sensitivity to smartphone price?

### Variables

- Monthly income
- Price importance
- Preference for less expensive alternatives

### Planned Analysis

Price-sensitivity measures will be compared across income groups.

Cross-tabulation and appropriate statistical testing will be used to evaluate whether price sensitivity differs systematically across income segments.

---

## Analysis 3 — Purchase Frequency and Product Priorities

### Research Question

Do consumers with different smartphone replacement frequencies assign different levels of importance to smartphone attributes?

### Variables

- Smartphone replacement frequency
- Smartphone purchase-factor importance scores

### Planned Analysis

Mean importance scores for key smartphone attributes will be compared across replacement-frequency groups.

ANOVA or another appropriate group-comparison method may be considered depending on the final distribution and assumptions of the data.

---

## Analysis 4 — Current Brand and Future Brand Preference

### Research Question

Is current smartphone brand associated with the likelihood of considering the same brand for the next purchase?

### Variables

- Current smartphone brand
- Future same-brand likelihood

### Planned Analysis

The relationship between current brand and future brand preference will be examined using cross-tabulation and appropriate statistical testing.

---

## Analysis 5 — Brand Satisfaction and Future Preference

### Research Question

Does satisfaction with the current smartphone brand relate to the likelihood of purchasing the same brand again?

### Variables

- Current brand satisfaction
- Future same-brand likelihood

### Planned Analysis

The relationship between brand satisfaction and future brand preference will be examined using descriptive comparison and an appropriate association/correlation method.

---

## Interpretation Approach

The analysis will distinguish between:

1. Descriptive differences between consumer groups
2. Statistical evidence of association
3. Practical business relevance

A difference between groups will not automatically be interpreted as statistically significant.

Statistical significance and practical relevance will be evaluated separately.

# Research Hypotheses

The following hypotheses are formulated based on the research questions and are intended to be evaluated using appropriate statistical methods after data collection.

## H1 — Price and Purchase Decision

**Alternative Hypothesis (H1):** Price is significantly associated with consumers' smartphone purchase decisions.

**Null Hypothesis (H0):** Price is not significantly associated with consumers' smartphone purchase decisions.

---

## H2 — Age and Brand Preference

**Alternative Hypothesis (H1):** Smartphone brand preference differs significantly across age groups.

**Null Hypothesis (H0):** Smartphone brand preference does not differ significantly across age groups.

---

## H3 — Income and Price Sensitivity

**Alternative Hypothesis (H1):** Consumers' income level is significantly associated with their sensitivity to smartphone price.

**Null Hypothesis (H0):** Consumers' income level is not significantly associated with their sensitivity to smartphone price.

---

## H4 — Online Reviews and Purchase Decisions

**Alternative Hypothesis (H1):** The importance consumers assign to online reviews is significantly associated with their smartphone purchase decisions.

**Null Hypothesis (H0):** The importance consumers assign to online reviews is not significantly associated with their smartphone purchase decisions.

---

## H5 — Purchase Frequency and Product Priorities

**Alternative Hypothesis (H1):** Consumers with different smartphone purchasing frequencies assign significantly different levels of importance to smartphone product attributes.

**Null Hypothesis (H0):** Consumers with different smartphone purchasing frequencies do not assign significantly different levels of importance to smartphone product attributes.

---

## H6 — Previous Brand Experience and Future Preference

**Alternative Hypothesis (H1):** Previous smartphone brand experience is significantly associated with consumers' future brand preferences.

**Null Hypothesis (H0):** Previous smartphone brand experience is not significantly associated with consumers' future brand preferences.

---

## Hypothesis Testing Approach

The appropriate statistical test will be selected after examining the structure and measurement scale of the collected data.

Potential methods may include:

- Chi-square test of independence for categorical variables
- Independent samples t-test for comparing two groups
- ANOVA for comparing more than two groups
- Correlation analysis for measuring relationships between numerical or ordinal variables
- Regression analysis where appropriate

A significance level of **α = 0.05** will be used as the default threshold for statistical significance.

The final statistical method will depend on the characteristics of the collected data and the assumptions of each test.

# Secondary Research: Smartphone Market Context

## Purpose

Before conducting primary research, existing market research was reviewed to understand the broader smartphone market and identify consumer trends that may influence purchasing decisions.

The secondary research provides context for the primary consumer survey and helps identify relevant factors that should be investigated further.

---

## 1. Indian Smartphone Market Overview

India represents a large and highly competitive smartphone market with significant variation across brands, price segments and product categories.

According to Counterpoint Research, India's smartphone market grew approximately 1% year-on-year in volume and 8% in value during 2025. The difference between volume and value growth indicates continued movement toward higher-value smartphones.

The premium smartphone segment, defined by Counterpoint as devices priced above INR 30,000, accounted for 22% of India's smartphone shipments in 2025 and recorded 11% year-on-year volume growth.

### Research Implication

The increasing importance of premium smartphones suggests that consumer purchasing decisions may not be driven by price alone. Product performance, brand perception, camera capabilities, financing options and other perceived-value factors may also influence purchase decisions.

---

## 2. Consumer Purchase Considerations

Existing consumer research provides evidence that product specifications and performance-related attributes are important considerations for Indian smartphone buyers.

A Counterpoint Research consumer survey published in January 2025 found that processor performance was a leading consideration in smartphone purchase decisions. The research also identified battery life, storage capacity and camera quality among important purchase considerations.

### Research Implication

These findings support the inclusion of performance-related attributes such as processor performance, battery life, storage and camera quality in the primary research questionnaire.

However, the present study will investigate a broader set of factors rather than assuming that existing findings apply equally to all consumers.

---

## 3. Competitive Market Structure

The Indian smartphone market contains several major brands competing across different price segments.

Counterpoint Research reported that vivo led India's smartphone market by shipment volume in 2025 with a 20% share, while Samsung and OPPO were among the other major brands. Apple led the market in value terms with a 28% share.

The market therefore includes brands with different positioning strategies, ranging from value-focused and mass-market offerings to premium products.

### Research Implication

Brand preference should be examined as part of the primary research because consumers may evaluate brands differently depending on factors such as price, perceived quality, performance and brand reputation.

---

## 4. Key Factors Identified for Further Investigation

Based on the secondary research and the research objectives, the following potential purchase factors will be investigated through primary research:

- Price
- Brand reputation
- Processor/performance
- Camera quality
- Battery life
- Storage capacity
- Design
- Display quality
- Online reviews
- Recommendations from friends and family
- Promotional offers and discounts
- After-sales service
- 5G/connectivity
- AI and smart features

The study will not assume that all factors have equal importance. Their relative importance will be measured using primary data.

---

## 5. Research Gap

Existing market research provides valuable information about smartphone market growth, brand performance and important product attributes.

However, secondary research alone does not answer the specific questions defined in this study, particularly:

- How do consumers rank different purchase factors relative to one another?
- Do purchase priorities differ across demographic groups?
- Is price sensitivity associated with income level?
- How important are online reviews and recommendations?
- Does previous brand experience influence future brand preference?
- Can consumers be grouped according to their purchase priorities?

The primary research component of this project is therefore designed to investigate these questions using consumer-level survey data.

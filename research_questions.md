# Research Questions

The study is designed to answer the following research questions:

## Primary Research Question

**RQ1. What are the most influential factors affecting consumers' smartphone purchase decisions?**

## Secondary Research Questions

**RQ2. How important are price, brand, performance, camera quality, battery life, design, storage, online reviews, promotional offers, and after-sales service in the smartphone purchase decision?**

**RQ3. Do smartphone purchase priorities differ across different age groups and other demographic segments?**

**RQ4. Is there an association between consumers' income levels and their sensitivity to smartphone price?**

**RQ5. How strongly do online reviews and recommendations influence consumers' smartphone purchase decisions?**

**RQ6. Do consumers with different smartphone purchasing frequencies prioritize different product attributes?**

**RQ7. Does previous smartphone ownership or brand experience influence consumers' future brand preferences?**

**RQ8. Can consumers be grouped into distinct segments based on their smartphone purchase priorities and preferences?**

**RQ9. What implications do the identified consumer preferences have for smartphone product positioning, marketing, and customer targeting?**

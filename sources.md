# Secondary Research Sources

The following sources were reviewed to establish the market context for this research project.

---

## Source 1 — Counterpoint Research

**Title:** Over One in Every Five Smartphones Shipped in India is Now Premium; Apple Records its Highest Ever Value Share

**Publisher:** Counterpoint Research

**Publication Date:** February 2, 2026

**URL:** https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/Premiumization-Gains-Strength-in-India-Smartphone-Market-in-2025

**Key Information Used:**

- India's smartphone market grew 1% YoY in volume in 2025.
- Market value grew 8% YoY.
- Smartphones priced above INR 30,000 accounted for 22% of shipments.
- Premiumization continued to influence the market.

**Relevance to This Study:**

The findings provide context for understanding the changing price structure of the Indian smartphone market and the potential importance of perceived value in purchase decisions.

---

## Source 2 — Counterpoint Research

**Title:** Survey: Smartphone Consumers Willing to Pay More for a Better Processor

**Publisher:** Counterpoint Research

**Publication Date:** January 27, 2025

**URL:** https://www.counterpointresearch.com/insight/survey-smartphone-consumers-willing-to-pay-more-for-a-better-processor/

**Key Information Used:**

- Processor performance was identified as a leading purchase consideration.
- Battery life, storage capacity and camera quality were also important considerations.
- The research examined consumers' willingness to pay for specific smartphone features.

**Relevance to This Study:**

The findings support the inclusion of performance, battery, storage and camera-related factors in the primary research questionnaire.

## Source 3 — Counterpoint Research

**Title:** India Smartphone Market Share: Quarterly

**Publisher:** Counterpoint Research

**Publication Date:** May 28, 2025

**URL:** https://www.counterpointresearch.com/insights/india-smartphone-share/

The competitive landscape provides context for examining brand preference and previous brand experience as potential purchase factors.

## Source Evaluation

The sources selected for this stage are industry research sources rather than informal blogs or user-generated content. They are used to establish market context and identify areas for further investigation.

The findings from these sources will not be treated as findings from the primary survey conducted in this project.

# Research Objectives

## Primary Objective

To identify and evaluate the key factors that influence consumers when making smartphone purchase decisions.

## Secondary Objectives

1. To determine the relative importance of factors such as price, brand, performance, camera quality, battery life, design, storage, online reviews, promotional offers, and after-sales service.

2. To understand whether smartphone purchase priorities vary across different consumer demographic segments.

3. To examine the relationship between consumers' price sensitivity and their purchasing preferences.

4. To assess the influence of online reviews and recommendations on smartphone purchase decisions.

5. To identify differences in purchase preferences based on consumers' previous smartphone ownership and purchasing behavior.

6. To identify the major consumer segments based on their smartphone purchase priorities and preferences.

7. To generate actionable insights that can help smartphone companies improve product positioning, marketing communication, and customer targeting.
